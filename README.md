# 💰 SpendIt

**Plan your spending before it happens, not after.**

A beautiful iOS app that helps you take control of your money by planning where it should go each month.

---

## What It Does

SpendIt helps you answer: *"Can I afford this?"* **before** you spend.

- Create monthly spending plans
- Add your income, expenses, and savings goals
- See instantly if your plan balances
- Freeze items to explore "what-if" scenarios
- Beautiful, modern design that's actually fun to use

---

## Why It's Different

Most apps track spending **after** you spend it. By then, it's too late.

SpendIt helps you **decide** where your money goes, so you stay in control.

---
