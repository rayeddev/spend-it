# Privacy Policy

**Effective: 2026-05-07**

SpendIt is built with privacy as a feature. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing on our servers.** We have no servers.

The only data SpendIt creates is what you create yourself inside the app: spending plans, items (income, outcomes, savings), names, amounts, and notes you choose to write.

## Where your data lives

Your data is stored in two places, both controlled by you:

1. **On your device**, in iOS's local storage.
2. **In your private iCloud account**, via Apple's CloudKit. This is the same iCloud account that backs up your other Apple data. SpendIt syncs to a private database that only you can access — not the developer, not Apple, not anyone else.

If you sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud for SpendIt in Settings, your data stays on your device only.

## What we don't do

- We do not collect analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or usage data.
- We do not use third-party SDKs that track you (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Crashlytics, no advertising networks).
- We do not show ads.
- We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to anyone.
- We do not have user accounts, login systems, or any way to identify you.

## Permissions

SpendIt does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, calendar, or any other system feature.

## Children

SpendIt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we don't collect or store anything, there is nothing to request, export, or delete from us. To remove all SpendIt data:

- Delete the app from your device, and
- Open **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpendIt** to remove iCloud data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the effective date above will be updated. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
